## Private original baseline, kept separately

The actual later AI Demos research reports, accepted task verdicts, researcher
feedback and original failures were reviewed and mapped privately. Their case
details, unpublished artifacts, discussions and aggregate counts are not
approved public research here. They are not exported into this directory.

**Not publicly verifiable** means the exact case/configuration or release
permission is unavailable publicly. It does not replace an internally recorded
Pass/Fail/unset result. A later maintainer fixture is separate validation.
Historical Pass controls and unresolved Fail/uncertain cases remain retained.

No case-level benchmark upgrade, aggregate pass rate or score comparison is
claimed. In particular, relative-date handling and semantic follow-up fidelity
are not solved merely by honest unavailable answers or working stacked charts.

## How a future result becomes comparable

Record the original source/run, literal verdict, fixture bytes or same-session
source fingerprint, question and business definition, SQL/reference result,
model/configuration, commit, environment, attempts and complete rows. For a
changed source database, grade against the data actually queried rather than
historical numeric literals. If the old schema/version is missing, preserve that
unknown and label the new replay accordingly. See [maintenance](MAINTAINING.md).
